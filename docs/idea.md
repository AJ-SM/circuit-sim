The Idea
Think of it as the notes app already built into every smartboard — but reimagined as a web platform, with everything that app is missing. Professors already open a notes app on the smartboard to write and draw during lecture; Smartyfyp takes that exact same familiar habit and quietly upgrades every part of it — turning flat strokes into structured, intelligent content, without asking the professor to change how they teach.
A professor draws a circuit, speaks through it, or uploads an image — and the platform automatically turns that into a live, working simulation students can play with, alongside clean, organized notes from the same lecture, all in one shareable page.
Key Features
Familiar, smartboard-style writing and drawing surface — but on the web, accessible from any device, not locked to one physical board in one classroom.
Draw, speak, or upload — your choice. Sketch a circuit live, describe it aloud, or upload a photo/PDF — handled the same way.
Automatically structured notes, unlike a plain smartboard notes app — raw handwriting or speech becomes organized notes with headings, key terms, and properly formatted formulas, with zero manual tidying.
Real-time sketch-to-circuit visualization — a hand-drawn circuit is automatically recognized and converted into a proper, simulate-able schematic as it's drawn.
Live, interactive simulation — students can drag component values and watch the circuit's actual behavior update instantly, not just see a static drawing.
Solve-for-unknown support — state a condition aloud (“find Rx so the bridge is balanced”) and the system solves it, showing the answer directly on the circuit.
One shareable, persistent lecture page — unlike a smartboard session that's gone once the class ends, every lecture's notes and live circuit are saved, linked, and accessible anytime.
Benefits
For Students
See exactly how a circuit behaves, not just its formula-based answer — building real intuition, not memorization.
Never lose a lecture again — every session is saved, structured, and revisitable, unlike writing that disappears off a physical board.
Experiment freely with values and see the effect instantly, with no need to learn LTspice/Multisim separately.
For Professors
Keep teaching exactly the way they already do — draw and talk on a familiar notes-app-style surface — with nothing new to learn.
Get organized, shareable notes automatically, instead of manually typing up what was written on the board after class.
No time spent manually recreating circuits in separate simulation software.
For the Institution
Modernizes the exact tool professors already use daily, rather than asking them to adopt something unfamiliar.
Makes core ECE understanding (circuit behavior, not just formulas) more consistent and accessible across every classroom.
Every lecture becomes a reusable, shareable institutional resource instead of a one-time board session.
